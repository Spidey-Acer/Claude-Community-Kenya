Entry & Exit Animations — Pick Your Drama Level

These describe what happens when the persona selector appears and when you pick a side. The bolder the animation, the more memorable the first impression.

A

The Curtain Call

Entry: Screen starts fully black. A thin glowing line appears dead center (vertical). It pulses once, then the two halves "peel apart" like curtains — dev side slides left revealing green particles/scanlines underneath, pro side slides right revealing warm amber glow. Each side's content fades in with a stagger (icon first, title, subtitle, preview text). Total: ~1.2s

Exit: The side you DIDN'T pick dissolves into particles that drift toward your chosen side (like it's absorbing the other). Your side then "zooms in" — scales up to fill the screen while its accent color briefly flashes the whole viewport, then fades to reveal the actual site underneath. Total: ~1s

B

The Matrix Reveal

Entry: Screen fills with your existing Matrix rain effect (green characters falling). The rain splits down the middle — left side stays green (dev), right side characters morph to amber (pro). Rain gradually clears from center outward, revealing each half's content behind it like a digital fog lifting. Total: ~1.5s

Exit: On click, the chosen side's rain intensifies and sweeps across the rejected side like a wave, "overwriting" it. Screen fills with the winning color's rain, then the characters dissolve downward revealing the homepage. Total: ~1.2s

C

The Terminal Boot

Entry: Black screen. A blinking cursor appears center-top. It "types out" — initializing cck_experience... then detecting user profile... then two modes available: — each line appearing with typing sound rhythm. Then BOOM — the two halves slam in from left and right edges simultaneously, meeting at center with a brief impact flash on the divider line. Content staggers in. Total: ~2s

Exit: The rejected side "crashes" — its content glitches (your existing glitch CSS), scrambles, then collapses downward like a terminal window closing. The chosen side smoothly expands, its border glow intensifying, then transitions seamlessly into the actual page layout. Total: ~1s

D

Mix & Match

Take entry from one, exit from another. Tell me which pieces you like and I'll combine them. Or describe something completely different — I'll make it work.
