👻 Ambient (no modal)

Zero friction. No splash. Hero says "I'm a [developer]" with the role as an inline chip — click to change. Page below silently adapts. Nothing blocks the user. Lowest commitment, lowest signal.

My recommendation

Lead with the AI-guided conversation as the headline experience — there's no other community on earth with as strong a reason to do this (you're literally a Claude community, so Claude greeting you is meta-perfect). Then borrow from Ambient for returning visitors — never block someone twice. The chat collects audience + intent in 2-3 messages, sets persona, and routes them to Discord with a personalized invite.

⚠️ Tradeoff: AI conversation needs an LLM API call (cheap with Haiku 4.5 — ~$0.001/visitor) and careful prompt design to stay fast. We'd cache common flows and stream responses. Or do it 100% client-side with pre-scripted branches if cost matters.
