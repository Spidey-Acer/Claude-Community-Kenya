Audience layer ≠ Skin layer
Audience drives content & entry experience. Skin (Dev/Pro) is independent — any persona can pick either mood. Two orthogonal axes.

Hybrid: deep entry, shared core

Cost: ~2.5x current. Risk: medium. Each audience gets a tailored welcome + personalized content, but core site stays unified. Skin (Dev/Pro) becomes a separate "vibe" toggle.

My recommendation

The Hybrid approach. You get the "feels made for me" magic of Deep without the 5x maintenance tax. The trick: separate who you are (audience → content) from how you like things to look (skin → aesthetic). A founder might love the terminal aesthetic; a developer might prefer polished. Don't conflate them.
